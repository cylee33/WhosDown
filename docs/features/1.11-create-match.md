## Goal

A Player hosts a Match in three short steps (essentials → Venue and details → review) and publishes it. It immediately shows up for nearby Players.

The Host's own RSVP and the conversation can be created only in the same batch, by the Host.

## Rules and edge cases

1. **Required:** sport, date, start and end time, location, player limit, join type. Everything else is optional.
2. **Times:** start must be in the future; end after start; length up to 6 hours. Default: next full hour, 90 minutes.
3. **Player limit:** 2–50, counting the Host (see Open questions).
4. **Price:** Free, or Paid with an amount up to $100. Display only; no payments.
5. **Custom sport:** "+ Add custom activity" sets `sport: "other"` and a `customSportName` (1–30 characters).
6. **Title:** see Open questions.
7. **Waitlist:** see Open questions.
8. Venue facts (indoor, surface, lights) are copied from the Venue onto the Match.
9. Back from H18 or H19 keeps everything typed. Leaving the Create tab keeps the draft until publish or app restart.
10. Publish button is disabled while publishing (no double Matches). On failure: "Couldn't publish. Check your connection and try again."
11. After publish, the Match appears in Discover for others, and in the Host's Hosting list (1.19).
12. Guests tapping the Create tab get the Guest gate (1.3).

## Open questions

1. **Does the Host count toward the player limit?** H11 counts the Host in "12 / 14 joined", but H45 shows "0 / 14 joined" right after publishing. _Suggested:_ the Host counts. `joinedCount` starts at 1, and H45 shows "1 / 14 joined".
2. **Match title:** H17 has no title field, but Matches have titles ("7v7 at Schenley"). _Suggested:_ an optional title field on H17, defaulting to "Pickup {Sport}".
3. **Waitlist switch:** H19 shows "Waitlist ON", but no screen has a switch. _Suggested:_ a "Waitlist when full" switch on H17 next to player limit, on by default.
