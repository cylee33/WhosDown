## Goal

A Player sees everything about one Match: what, when, where, how far, conditions, who is going, and who hosts it. They see it update live.

## Rules and edge cases

1. Everything updates live (counts, edits, cancellation).
2. Chips: "Free" or price; "Open" or "Approval"; skill level.
3. Venue box shows only the facts that are set; "Field reserved 6:45–8:45" only when `fieldReserved`.
4. "2 spots left" = `playerLimit − joinedCount`; "Full" when 0.
5. The whole Host row is tappable and opens the Host's profile (H10).
6. **Action area** is decided by `JoinRules` (1.13/1.14) and the RSVP: Join / Request to join / Full · Join waitlist / status card / Guest gate. For the Host: "Manage Match", opening H20 (1.15).
7. **••• menu:** "Report Match" (1.25), and "Add to calendar" for approved Players and the Host (1.23).
8. **Ended Match:** a note "This Match has ended and moved to Past Matches", and no action buttons.
9. **Cancelled Match:** red banner "Cancelled: <reason>", and no action buttons.
10. Opening a Match whose document is gone shows "This Match is no longer available" and goes back.
11. Before the Match starts, a note under the actions: "Matches move to Past Matches after they end."
