## Goal

A Player creates an account with email and password, signs in, resets a forgotten password, and signs out.

## Data

| ID | Read / write | Path and fields |
|---|---|---|
| Auth | — | `createUser(withEmail:password:)`, `signIn(withEmail:password:)`, `sendPasswordReset(withEmail:)`, `signOut()`, auth-state listener |
| W1 | write (batch) | `users/{uid}`: `displayName`, `displayNameLowercase`, `createdAt`, `onboardedAt: null`. `users/{uid}/private/settings`: `email`, `searchRadiusMiles: 3` |
| R7 | read | `users/{uid}`: `onboardedAt`, to decide where to route after sign-in |

**Security rules this feature needs:**

- `users/{uid}` is created only by that uid, with `displayName` 1–40 characters.
- `private/settings` is created only by its owner.

## Rules and edge cases

1. After "Continue" on H02: create the Auth user, write W1, then go to onboarding (1.4).
2. After sign-in: if `onboardedAt` is null, resume onboarding; otherwise open the tab bar on Discover.
3. Password: at least 8 characters; show the rule under the field.
4. Firebase errors show as plain sentences under the field ("That email is already in use. Sign in instead?", "Wrong email or password.", "No internet connection."). Never show raw error codes.
5. "Forgot password?" sends the reset email for the typed address and always shows "If an account exists for that email, we sent a reset link", whether or not one exists.
6. The Continue and Sign in buttons are disabled while a request is running, so a double tap can't create two accounts.
7. Sign-out returns to H01 and clears in-memory state (view models, listeners).
8. The app remembers the session; reopening the app skips H01.
9. Email verification isn't required in V1.
