## Goal

A Player who is going to a Match adds it to their phone's calendar in two taps.

## Screens

| Screen | What this feature owns on it |
|---|---|
| H14 | "Calendar" button in "Match-day coordination" |
| H09 ••• menu | "Add to calendar" (approved Players and the Host) |
| System sheet | iOS "New Event" editor, filled in |

## Data

| Source | What |
|---|---|
| EventKit | `EKEventEditViewController` with an `EKEvent` |

Uses the Match already on screen plus one repository lookup of its saved Venue for the address: V1 Match snapshots contain `venueName` and coordinates but no address. No Firestore writes. If the Venue cannot be loaded, the event still includes the Venue name.

## Rules and edge cases

1. Event title: "{Match title} · WhosDown"; start and end from the Match; location: Venue name and address; notes: the Host's notes.
2. Available to approved Players and the Host, for Matches that haven't started.
3. The Player can change anything in the system sheet before saving.
4. The app doesn't remember or update the event; if the Match changes, the H51 notification tells the Player.
5. Cancelled in the sheet: nothing happens.
