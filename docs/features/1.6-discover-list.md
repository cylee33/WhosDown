## Goal

The same nearby Matches as the map, as a list a Player can scan: time, place, distance, level, spots, and price.

## Screens

| Screen | What this feature owns on it |
|---|---|
| H07 Discover list | Title "Matches near you"; Map/List toggle; quick chips (sport, "Tonight", distance, "Filters N"); rows: sport icon, title, time · Venue · distance, level chip, joined/limit, price |

## Data

Same as 1.5 (R1, R8). No new reads or writes.

## Rules and edge cases

1. Sorted by start time, soonest first. Matches happening now come first, labelled "Now".
2. Row shows: title; "7:00 PM · CMU Soccer Field · 0.4 mi"; level chip; "12/14"; "Free" or the price ("$5").
3. Full Matches show "Full" in place of the count, colored like the waitlist status.
4. Quick chips are shortcuts into the same filters as 1.7; "Filters N" shows how many filters are on and opens H08.
5. Tapping a row opens Match details (1.8).
6. Pull to refresh re-runs R1.
7. Empty state matches 1.5 rule 9.
8. Dates beyond today read "Tomorrow · 6:00 PM" or "Sat, Nov 8 · 6:00 PM", in the phone's time zone (D42).
