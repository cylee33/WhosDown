## Goal

A Host finds the field, court, or gym for their Match, either a saved Venue or any place on the map. A new place is saved as a Venue for everyone.

**Security rules this feature needs:** any signed-in non-Guest can create a Venue with `createdBy == auth.uid`; only the creator can update it; nobody deletes.

## Rules and edge cases

1. Suggested list = saved Venues near the Host, nearest first. Typing filters it and adds MapKit results below, under "Other places".
2. Choosing a MapKit place that is within 50 m of a saved Venue with a similar name uses the saved Venue (no duplicates).
3. A new place asks three quick questions in a small sheet before saving: Indoor or Outdoor, surface (turf, grass, hardwood, court, sand, other), and lights (yes/no).
4. The bottom button names the choice: "Use CMU Soccer Field". Disabled until something is chosen.
5. Without location permission, search still works, centered on CMU.
6. No network: show saved results already loaded, and "Search needs a connection."
