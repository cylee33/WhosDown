## Goal

A Player sees all their Matches in one tab: what's coming up and their status for each, what they host, and what's done.

## Rules and edge cases

1. **Upcoming:** my RSVPs that are approved, pending, waitlisted, or declined on Matches that haven't ended, soonest first. `left` RSVPs aren't shown.
2. **Hosting:** Matches I host that haven't ended, soonest first. Tapping opens H20 (1.15).
3. **Past:** Matches that ended or were cancelled where I was approved or hosted, newest first, grouped by month.
4. A Match moves to Past when its `endTime` passes, while the app is open (re-check every minute).
5. Badges use the shared `StatusBadge` colors (1.14).
6. Past-row badge for a Player: "Attended" or "Didn't attend" if checked in, otherwise "Not checked in"; "Cancelled" for cancelled Matches. For the Host: "Hosted", plus "Check in" while open (1.27).
7. Month header counts Matches and attended ones for that month.
8. Tapping any row opens Match details (1.8).
9. Empty states: "No upcoming Matches. Find one on Discover." / "You're not hosting anything. Create a Match." / "No past Matches yet."
10. Guests see the Guest prompt (1.3).
