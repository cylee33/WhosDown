## Rules and edge cases

1. Reminder 1 hour before the start, for approved Matches and Matches I host (D26). No setting in V1.
2. Not for pending or waitlisted RSVPs. When approval arrives, the sync adds it.
3. A Match starting in less than an hour gets no reminder.
4. **Sync runs:** on app launch, when returning to the foreground, and when my RSVPs or hosted Matches change. Each sync adds missing reminders, moves changed ones, and removes reminders for left, declined, or cancelled Matches.
5. Tapping the reminder opens that Match's details.
6. Permission is asked once during onboarding (H05). Players who signed up as Guests and upgraded go through H05 too. Permission denied: no reminders, and no repeat prompts. Show "Reminders are off. Turn on notifications in Settings." once on H14.
7. A reminder is only on the devices that have synced. If the Player leaves on another device, the next sync here removes it.
